Generadas con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`, sin CLI ni imágenes externas. Los PNG conservan sus píxeles y transparencia originales. Blender mapea la palma completa y las cuatro regiones de hojas sobre superficies curvas; no se usan como fondos del juego.

## Palma

Archivo: `public/celta/palm-frond-v2.png`.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original photorealistic PBR foliage albedo cutout for a Blender game model. Create ONE complete botanically plausible African understory feather palm frond, laid flat, orthographic view directly from above, entire frond uncropped centered in a tall portrait canvas. Slender central rachis starts bottom center with a short bare stalk and extends to the top, organic subtle sideward sweep. About 28 irregularly offset tapered narrow leaflets on each side, naturally clustered asymmetrical, unequal lengths and spacing, some overlapping/curled and 3–5 damaged tips, NOT a regular comb. Overall silhouette elongated, length 2.5 times maximum width. Olive forest green mature living leaves with fine parallel veins, pale fine midrib, slight occasional yellow-green aging, a few tiny holes and frayed tips. Physically plausible natural subdued reflectance. Very soft neutral even overcast illumination, NO cast shadow, NO specular highlights, NO directional baked lighting, crisp detailed surface. Actual transparent background everywhere outside the plant including gaps between leaflets; PNG alpha, no background color or checkerboard. All leaf ends visible, at least 5% transparent margin. No text, labels, other plants, ground, pots, borders, logos or watermark. This is a game texture surface, not an illustration.

## Hojas

Archivo: `public/celta/broadleaf-atlas-v2.png`.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original photorealistic albedo texture atlas for separate sculpted 3D leaves in Blender, real PNG transparent background. Create exactly FOUR isolated mature African rainforest understory broad leaves arranged in a strict 2 by 2 grid, one complete leaf within each quadrant, generous transparent separation and margins. Each leaf has petiole at bottom middle of its quadrant, apex top, top-down orthographic flat scan. Top left: long lanceolate dark olive leaf, gently uneven edges and yellow-green thin central vein. Top right: wider elliptical medium olive leaf with slightly puckered surface, one naturally torn edge. Bottom left: asymmetric lanceolate deep forest green leaf, tiny insect holes, natural fine veins branching diagonally from midrib. Bottom right: curled-edge long elliptical lighter olive leaf with small brown dry tip and slight aging. Actual botanical realism, waxy but matte in diffuse light, clearly visible delicate venation and tiny freckles; no exaggerated gloss. No stylization. Each leaf length roughly 2.7 times its width, short thin petiole. Entire leaf visible, 10 percent clear padding in each quadrant. All leaves presented flat for UV texture use, no perspective, no cast shadows, soft even neutral overcast illumination without strong shading or baked highlights. Transparent alpha everywhere outside the leaves and in holes; no backdrop or checkerboard. No text, other objects, labels, borders or watermarks.
