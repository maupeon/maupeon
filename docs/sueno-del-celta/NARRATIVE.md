# El sueño del celta — narrative design

An independent, Spanish-language, interactive homage inspired by the three parts of Mario Vargas Llosa's novel: El Congo, La Amazonía and Irlanda. Dialogue and mission text are original dramatizations, not quotations. Anonymous witnesses and the roadside encounter are composite fictional encounters; the game must not imply that these exchanges are documented events. Historical notes and primary-source links are accessible from the notebook.

The novel's prison/remembered-journey structure informs short Pentonville interludes before each chapter. The player's choices change the notebook's interpretation, not historical outcomes. No score is awarded for suffering. No weapons, violence-as-entertainment or alternate survival ending. The fixed conclusion explicitly reports Casement's execution by hanging on 3 August 1916 at Pentonville, without a graphic depiction.

## Nine short missions

1. Congo, 1903: hear an anonymous witness, compare the quota ledger, dispatch the investigation (report published 1904).
2. Putumayo, 1910: hear testimony through an interpreter, compare company records and the testimony of Barbadian workers, prepare the report (published 1912; historical return in 1911 is explained).
3. Ireland, 1916: recover landing notes, try to communicate a warning about inadequate support for the rising, then write a final page in Pentonville after arrest and trial. These geographical/time transitions are explicitly signposted.

The final notebook preserves all nine choices. Restart is offered only through an explicit in-game confirmation. A completed game can be revisited to read the notebook and ending.
